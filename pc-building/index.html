About This Skill

I build custom PCs from the ground up, handling everything from component research and selection to full assembly, cable management, BIOS configuration, and post-build testing. My builds have been for personal use and family members, ranging from everyday setups to high-end gaming machines. I focus on choosing parts that work well together, keeping thermals under control, and making sure every build is reliable long-term.
