Season History

Into the Deep Season (2024-25)

Our first competitive FTC season. Competed in the Into the Deep challenge and finished 8th in our league as a rookie team, surpassing our initial expectations. This season established the team's foundation in robot design, driver practice, and competition strategy.

  • Finished 8th in league standings as a first-year team
  • Designed and built a competition-ready robot from scratch
  • Developed and refined driving strategy across multiple matches
  • Documented the full build process in the engineering notebook

DECODE Season (2025-26)

Our second season, returning with more experience and a stronger robot. Applied lessons from Into the Deep to improve mechanical reliability, autonomous performance, and competition driving. The Goddard Tournament results reflect that growth directly.

  • Finished 1st in qualifications at the Goddard Tournament with a 5-0-0 record
  • Improved robot design with a mecanum drivetrain and computer vision autonomous
  • Logged 300+ hours of build and practice time across the season
  • Continuing in league play with goals of advancing to regional and state championships

My Roles and Contributions

Team Captain

As team captain I organize meetings, keep the team on schedule through the season, make final decisions on robot design direction, and represent the team during competitions and judging sessions.

  • Coordinated team meetings and build sessions throughout both seasons
  • Led design discussions and made final calls on mechanical priorities
  • Managed communication between team members and mentors
  • Kept the engineering notebook organized and current

Lead Builder and Mechanical

Responsible for the physical construction of the robot, including the drivetrain, scoring mechanisms, and structural assembly. Handled iterative testing and rebuilding throughout both seasons.

  • Designed and assembled the mecanum wheel drivetrain
  • Built and tested intake and scoring mechanisms through multiple iterations
  • Selected motors, servos, and structural components based on game requirements
  • Performed quick repairs and adjustments between matches at competitions

Driver

Served as the primary driver during competition matches, responsible for executing the team's game strategy in real time. A 5-0-0 record at the Goddard Tournament reflects the consistency built through extensive practice.

  • Practiced driver control routines to build consistency before competitions
  • Executed game strategy under competition pressure across both seasons
  • Adapted driving approach in real time based on alliance partners and opponents
  • Coordinated with the programmer on autonomous and driver-control transitions
