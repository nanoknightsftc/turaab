DIAGNOSTIC PROCESS

// methodology.sys

Every troubleshooting job follows the same general approach regardless of the system or symptom.

  1. 01
    STEP [ 1 ]
    Understand the Symptom
    When does it happen, how often, and what changed recently. Getting a clear picture of the problem before touching anything.
  2. 02
    STEP [ 2 ]
    Check the Obvious First
    Connections, seating, power delivery, and any visible physical damage. The simplest explanations are the most common.
  3. 03
    STEP [ 3 ]
    Isolate Variables
    Test components individually to narrow down the source. One change at a time until the failure point is clear.
  4. 04
    STEP [ 4 ]
    Use Diagnostic Tools
    MemTest86, CrystalDiskInfo, HWiNFO, Prime95, FurMark, and Windows Event Viewer. Let the data confirm what the symptom suggests.
  5. 05
    STEP [ 5 ]
    Verify the Fix
    Stress test and monitor after the change to confirm the issue is fully resolved. The job isn't done until the system is stable.
