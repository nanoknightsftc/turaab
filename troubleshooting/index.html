My Diagnostic Process

Every troubleshooting job follows the same general approach regardless of the system or symptom.

  • Understand the symptom: When does it happen, how often, and what changed recently
  • Check the obvious first: Connections, seating, power, and any visible physical issues
  • Isolate variables: Test components individually to narrow down the source
  • Use diagnostic tools: MemTest86, CrystalDiskInfo, HWiNFO, Prime95, FurMark, and Event Viewer
  • Verify the fix: Stress test and monitor after the change to confirm the issue is fully resolved
