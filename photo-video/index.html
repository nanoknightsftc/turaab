Photography & Videography

Event photography, videography, and post-production for Ismaili IComms

About This Skill

I volunteer with the Ismaili IComms team, the institutional communications team responsible for photography, videography, and social media content for the Ismaili community. I have shot and edited content for a range of events including community dinners, fundraisers, religious ceremonies, Dandiya celebrations, and other community gatherings. My role covers everything from shooting on location to post-production editing for final delivery.

IComms Experience

Event Photography and Videography

Shot photo and video content across a range of community events for the Ismaili IComms team, producing content used on official social media channels and internal communications.

  • Community dinners and fundraising events
  • Religious ceremonies and observances
  • Dandiya and cultural celebration events
  • General community gatherings and programs

Video Editing and Post-Production

Edited event footage and photos for final delivery, handling color grading, cuts, and export for social media formats using Adobe Premiere Pro and Lightroom.

  • Color grading and exposure correction in Adobe Lightroom and Premiere Pro
  • Cutting and sequencing event footage for social media posts
  • Exporting in formats optimized for Instagram, YouTube, and internal use
  • Organizing and managing raw files across a multi-drive storage setup

Equipment

Camera and Recording

  • Nikon Z7 - Full-frame mirrorless camera, primary shooting body
  • Nikkor 24-70mm f/4 S - Versatile zoom lens for events and general shooting
  • DJI RS4 Mini - Gimbal stabilizer for smooth handheld video
  • DJI Mic Mini - Wireless microphone system for clean audio capture
  • Flash unit - Supplemental lighting for indoor events
  • 3 batteries and charger - Extended shooting capacity for full-day events
  • IEMs - In-ear monitors for real-time audio monitoring during recording

Editing Workstation

Editing is done on my custom-built PC, the same machine listed on the PC Building page, with specs chosen in part for video rendering and Lightroom performance.

  • AMD Ryzen 7 9700X - 8-core processor for fast export and rendering
  • AMD Radeon RX 9070 XT - GPU-accelerated rendering in Premiere Pro
  • 32GB DDR5 6000MHz - Smooth performance with large project files
  • 1TB Gen 4 NVMe - OS and active project storage
  • 3x 1TB SATA SSD + 4TB HDD - Media library and archive storage

Storage and Backup

  • NAS system - Network-attached storage for file management and access
  • 2x 500GB drives in RAID 1 - Mirrored redundancy for important project files
  • 1.5TB available NAS storage - Ongoing project capacity

Software

  • Adobe Premiere Pro - Primary video editing and export
  • Adobe Lightroom - Photo editing and color grading
  • Adobe Photoshop - Advanced image compositing and retouching
